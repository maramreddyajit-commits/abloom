## Description

Abloom is a gentle companion for living with OCD. Each time you resist a compulsion, you water a hand-drawn plant. Over time it grows from a seedling into full bloom.

**When an urge hits**
Tap "i have an urge". Name it, then choose to resist it, delay it, or do a smaller version. A timer and short prompts help you ride the wave without needing to feel certain. Every option counts, so there's no all-or-nothing.

**Practise on purpose**
Build an exposure ladder from easiest to hardest. Abloom suggests your next step and guides you through each exposure. Make a prediction, notice your anxiety rise and fall, and write down what you learned.

**A plant that never wilts**
Your plant grows on the days you practise. Miss a day and it simply waits for you. There are no streaks to lose, and a calm mode hides every number if counting gets sticky.

**Learn the loop**
A one-minute read each day for your first week: how the OCD cycle works, why reassurance keeps it going, and how "maybe, maybe not" can help.

**Talk it through (optional)**
An AI companion helps you name compulsions and spot thinking traps. It's designed never to give reassurance, because reassurance feeds OCD.

**Private by design**
No account. What you log stays on your phone. Export a backup or share a plain summary with your therapist whenever you choose.

**When it's more than an urge**
Crisis lines are one tap away from every screen.

Abloom is a self-help tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It isn't a replacement for professional care. If OCD is affecting your life, consider talking to a healthcare professional; an ERP-trained therapist is the gold standard. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (US) or your local emergency number.
