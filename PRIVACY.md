# Abloom Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 10, 2026. Draft. Have it reviewed before you publish, and fill in the bracketed parts._

Abloom ("we", "the app") is a free self-help companion for people living with OCD. It was built to need as little of your data as possible.

## The short version

- **No account.** We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
- **What you log stays on your phone.** Your compulsions, waterings, exposure ladder, exposure sessions, notes and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
- **The AI companion is optional.** If you turn it on, the messages you type in that chat are sent to our server and on to Anthropic to generate a reply. Nothing else from the app is sent with them.
- **Analytics are off unless you opt in.** If you opt in, we receive anonymous usage counts (like "a plant was watered"), never what you log or type.
- **We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.**

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter in Abloom, including:

- your list of compulsions
- waterings, urge ratings and timers
- your exposure ladder, exposure sessions, predictions and what you learned
- your plant and forest
- your settings

This data is stored using your device's app storage. We can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it, as does **you → delete all my data**.

You can export a copy with **you → back up my data**, and share a summary with **share a summary**. These only go where you choose to send them.

## The AI companion (optional)

The companion only works after you agree on the consent screen in the **talk** tab. When you send a message:

- **What's sent:** the text of your current conversation, meaning your messages and the companion's replies in that chat. No other app data is included, and no identifiers like a name, email, device ID or advertising ID.
- **Where it goes:** to our server, a Cloudflare Worker that we operate. The server forwards the conversation to Anthropic, PBC, which provides the Claude AI model, to generate a reply.
- **Storage:**
  - Our server does not store your conversations.
  - Cloudflare may briefly process request metadata, such as your IP address, to deliver the request and to enforce rate limits.
  - Anthropic processes the conversation under its commercial API terms. [Confirm Anthropic's current API data retention and training terms and summarise them here.]
- **Reports:** if you tap **report** on a reply, we receive that reply's text and the reason you picked. This helps us improve safety. It does not include your messages.
- **Turning it off:** you can turn the companion off at any time in **you → ai companion**. The rest of the app works without it.

Please don't include names, addresses or other details that could identify you or anyone else.

## Anonymous analytics (optional, off by default)

If you switch on **help improve abloom**, the app sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, [PostHog, Inc.].

- **Events:** for example "onboarding completed", "plant watered", "exposure completed" or "lesson read".
- **Details sent with events:** a few numbers or categories, such as which of resist, delay or shrink was chosen, or an urge rating from 0–10. Also a random ID created on your device, your platform (iOS, Android or web) and the app version.
- **Never sent:** the names of your compulsions, anything you type, your chat, or your ladder.

You can switch analytics off at any time in **you → help improve abloom**.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally on your phone. No push server is involved.

## Children

Abloom is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

## Health information

Abloom is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. What you log about your OCD stays on your device, as described above. We never use health-related information for advertising or marketing.

## Your choices and rights

- Because we don't hold your app data, you control it directly. You can export it or delete it in the app.
- For questions about the AI companion or analytics data, or to make a privacy request under applicable law (for example the GDPR or CCPA), contact us at [support@abloom.app].

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for meaningful changes, let you know in the app.
